# LIVEOLATOR — Starter Content Plan (Week 1) + Templates

Goal for this stage: **downloads + feedback + an owned audience.** Everything below is
built to show the one idea that sells this app: *the visuals move with the music, and one
person plays both.* Show it, don't describe it.

> Before your first post: set up the accounts you don't have yet (IG, TikTok, YouTube),
> and put `liveolator.zalmanim.com` in every bio. That link is where downloads happen.

---

## The one asset that makes all of this work

**A 10–20 second screen recording of a track dropping and the visuals snapping to the
beat.** Capture a few of these. Almost every post this week reuses that footage. If you
only do one thing, do this. No footage = no visual product marketing.

Tip: record at a moment where the beat is obvious and the visual reaction is dramatic —
that's your hook. The first 1 second decides whether anyone watches.

## Templates you can reuse forever

### Instagram / TikTok caption template
```
Hook line (what they see in second 1). e.g. "Watch the visuals hit the drop 👇"

One sentence on why it's different: DJ + VJ in one app, running off the same beat clock —
so the visuals stay in time automatically.

Free on Windows. Link in bio.
#VJ #VJing #DJ #livevisuals #visualsforDJs #resolume #TouchDesigner #DJsoftware #livemusic
```
Rules: hook first, keep it short, one CTA, let the video do the talking.

### YouTube video template
```
TITLE (search-friendly + curiosity):
  "This FREE app is a DJ and a VJ at the same time (Windows)"

DESCRIPTION:
  LIVEOLATOR runs a DJ engine and a VJ engine off one shared beat clock, so your visuals
  stay locked to the music and one person can play both.

  It's free, Windows-only for now, and in active development — I'd love your feedback.

  ⬇️ Download (free): https://liveolator.zalmanim.com
  💬 Tell me what to build next: [your feedback link / Discord]

  Chapters:
  0:00 The problem (visuals out of time)
  0:20 One shared clock
  0:50 DJ side
  1:30 VJ side
  2:20 It's free — go try it

THUMBNAIL IDEA: split screen — waveform on one side, reactive visual on the other, big
word "FREE".
```

### Reddit / forum template (community-safe — read the subreddit rules first)
```
TITLE: I built a free app that DJs and VJs at the same time (one shared beat clock) —
       looking for feedback

BODY:
  Hey r/VJing — I've been building a free Windows app called LIVEOLATOR. The idea: run a
  DJ engine and a VJ engine off the *same* beat clock, so the visuals stay in time with
  the music automatically and one person can play both without a second laptop.

  It's early and definitely has rough edges — that's exactly why I'm posting. I'd really
  value feedback from people who actually VJ.

  [short clip of the visuals hitting a drop]

  It's completely free (no catch, no paid tier right now). Download's here if you want to
  try it: liveolator.zalmanim.com

  Happy to answer anything, and I'm taking feature requests seriously.
```
Never post the same text across many subreddits, never hide that you made it, and
contribute to the community before/after you post. If a sub bans self-promo, don't post —
comment helpfully instead and let people find you.

### App update → launch post template (use every time you ship a version)
```
IG/TikTok: "New in LIVEOLATOR: [feature] 👀" + clip of the feature in action + link in bio.
YouTube:   short "What's new in v[X]" walkthrough.
Reddit:    only if the update is genuinely notable to that community; frame as progress +
           thanks for earlier feedback.
Website:   changelog entry (you already have /changelog).
```

## What NOT to do
- Don't lead with text or feature lists — lead with footage.
- Don't claim Mac support (Windows only for now).
- Don't oversell or hide that it's early — candor is your edge with this crowd.
- Don't spray Reddit with copy-paste posts — that gets you banned and burns the brand.
- Don't chase follower counts; chase downloads, comments, and feedback.
