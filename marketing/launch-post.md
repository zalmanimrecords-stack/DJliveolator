## Body

Most setups bolt visuals onto a second machine and *hope* they stay in time. LIVEOLATOR
takes a different route: the DJ engine and the VJ engine run off the **same beat clock**.
So the visuals are beat-synced by construction, not by hand — and one person, on one rig,
can perform the music **and** the visuals at once.

**Watch it instead of reading about it:** [embed the drop clip here — the 10–20s screen
recording of a track dropping and the visuals snapping to the beat].

### What it does

- **One shared clock.** Audio and visuals lock together automatically. No second laptop,
  no second operator, no hand-syncing.
- **A real-time visual engine.** Images, video clips, and live camera/capture composited
  in beat-synced layers with GPU effects — a modern, Resolume-style feel.
- **A DJ engine that gets out of your way.** One-button tempo sync and auto-mix assist are
  there *on purpose*: they free your hands and attention so you can play the visuals.

### Being honest about where it's at

- **Windows only** right now (macOS is planned, not shipped — so we're not marketing it yet).
- **Early and in active development** — expect rough edges and frequent updates.
- **Not a deep pro-DJ tool.** If you need Serato-level DJ depth, that's deliberately not us.
  The DJ side is intentionally simple so the visual side can shine.

That candor is the point: this is a maker sharing a tool with the people who'll use it, and
it's shaped by their feedback.
